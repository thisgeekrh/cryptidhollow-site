Cryptid Hollow

A cozy farming game where your neighbors are cryptids.

Bigfoot runs the general store, Mothman delivers the mail, and Nessie lives in the pond out back. Grow crops, fill orders and fix up your Great-Aunt Millie's farmhouse in a little town full of legends.

A letter from Millie

Dear one,

If you're reading this, the farm is yours. The porch sags, the roof leaks, and there's a raccoon named Gerald who thinks he pays rent. He does not.

The neighbors are a little unusual. Bigfoot will take good care of you, as long as you don't point a camera at him. Mothman brings the mail, mostly to the right house. And if the Jersey Devil ever mentions five dollars, change the subject.

With love (and a few unpaid IOUs),
Great-Aunt Mildred "Millie" Mudwhistle

P.S. Fix up the house one room at a time. I hid a note in every corner.

Life in the Hollow

Grow your farm

Plant wheat, corn, pumpkins and more, then swipe across your fields to harvest. Raise chickens, cows and sheep, and turn your crops into bread, cheese and pie.

Fill Bigfoot's orders

The general store always needs something. Fill orders for coins, load Captain Kraken's ferry, and sell your extras to other farmers at the Roadside Stand.

Build workshops, gain neighbors

Every new building comes with a cryptid to run it. Each one has their own story, told a chapter at a time as you become friends.

Go fishing with Nessie

Cast a line in the pond, wait for the bobber to dip, and reel it in. Keep an eye out for the Golden Koi.

Decorate every corner

Lay paths, place lamps, plant flower beds and put up a Bigfoot statue. Move anything, anytime, and clear the overgrown woods to make room for more.

Festivals, derbies and a monthly pass

Seasonal festivals with prizes you can't get anywhere else, a weekly derby with your friends, and a new Hollow Pass every month with a reward at every step.

Fix up Millie's farmhouse

Millie left you a run-down shack. Patch it up one piece at a time, and each fix-up turns up another of her funny notes. It's a slow, cozy goal you'll keep coming back to.

And a lot of little surprises

  • Mothman's mail. A gift every day, a prophecy every week, and the occasional letter meant for someone else.
  • Gerald the raccoon. He borrows things from your barn. Catch him after dark and he'll give them back, with interest.
  • Seasons. Fall leaves, winter sweaters on your cows, and a costume week where everyone dresses up as someone else.
  • A coffee rivalry. The Owlman serves black coffee only. Lizard Man draws you latte art. They do not get along.
  • The five dollars. The Jersey Devil has owed Millie five dollars since 1974. He's paying it back. Five cents at a time.
  • Visit your friends. Share your farm with a code, help water a friend's fields, and send gifts back and forth.

Will my farm move to my new iPhone or iPad?

Yes. Your farm is saved in your iCloud account, so sign in with the same Apple Account and it comes along.

I bought Moonstones but didn't get them.

Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't fix it, email us.

How do I visit a friend's farm?

Tap the farmhouse, open the Friends tab, and enter their farm code.
