Cryptid Hollow

A cozy farming game where your neighbors are legends. By Jobin LLC.

Bigfoot runs the general store, Mothman delivers your mail and Nessie wants to go fishing. Grow crops, raise animals, fill orders and build the farm of your dreams in a hollow full of friendly cryptids.

Need help?

Email mattstrattdev@icloud.com and we'll get back to you. It helps to include your device, iOS version and what happened.

Common questions

Will my farm move to my new iPhone or iPad? Yes. Your farm is saved in your iCloud account, so sign in with the same Apple Account and it comes along.

I bought Moonstones but didn't get them. Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't fix it, email us.

How do I visit a friend's farm? Tap the farmhouse, open the Friends tab, and enter their farm code.
