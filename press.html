About the game

Cryptid Hollow is a cozy farming game set in a hollow where the neighbors are legends. Bigfoot runs the general store, Mothman delivers the mail, Nessie helps with the fishing and the Chupacabra makes the cheese. Players grow crops, raise animals, bake and craft goods, fill orders, befriend the cryptids and decorate the hollow at their own pace. There is no combat and nothing to lose.

Features

  • Farm, cook and craft across a mill, creamery, coop, barn and more
  • A cast of friendly cryptids to befriend, each with their own shop and stories
  • Fishing, a weekly derby with friends, a roadside stand and a ferry that takes goods to market
  • Visit and help friends' farms through Game Center
  • Decorate freely, with seasons, weather and a photo mode
  • Widgets and a Live Activity, iPhone and iPad support, VoiceOver labels and a Reduce Motion option

How it was made

Cryptid Hollow was made by one developer. The art is a mix of a licensed pixel art pack and original pieces, and AI tools were used to help build the game.
